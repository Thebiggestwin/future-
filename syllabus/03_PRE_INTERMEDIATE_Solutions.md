# 📚 PRE-INTERMEDIATE DARAJA — Solutions Pre-Intermediate
**Kitob:** Solutions Pre-Intermediate (Oxford)  
**Muddat:** 4 oy (16 hafta)  
**Dars jadvali:** Haftada 3 marta × 2 soat = Haftada 6 soat  
**Jami soat:** ~96 soat

---

## 🎯 Daraja maqsadi
O'quvchi bu darajani tugatgach:
- Turli mavzularda ishonch bilan suhbat qura oladi
- Present Perfect, Past Continuous, Conditionals (1-chi) ni to'g'ri ishlatadi
- 1000-1200 ta so'z boyligiga ega bo'ladi
- O'rta murakkablikdagi matnlarni o'qib tushunadi
- CEFR: A2 → B1 darajasiga yetadi

---

## 👨‍🏫 O'QITUVCHI UCHUN KO'RSATMALAR

1. **Present Perfect** — Bu darajaning eng qiyin mavzusi. 2-3 dars bag'ishlang
2. **Speaking:** Har darsda 20 daqiqa suhbat — debate va discussion mashqlari
3. **Writing:** Har haftada 1 ta paragraph (5-8 jumladan) — struktura o'rgatiladi
4. **Vocabulary:** Har darsda yangi so'zlar kontekstda o'rgatiladi — izohlama lug'at yuritiladi
5. **Listening:** Haftada 1 marta audio material — BBC Learning English ishlatish tavsiya etiladi
6. **Mock test:** 15-haftada to'liq mock test o'tkaziladi — o'quvchi tayyor bo'lishi uchun

---

## 📌 MUHIM ESLATMA
> Bu darajada o'quvchilar **yozma ko'nikma** (Writing) ni jiddiy o'rganishi kerak. Paragraph tuzilishi (Topic sentence → Support → Conclusion) har darsda eslatilsin. Bu Intermediate va undan yuqori darajalar uchun asos bo'ladi.
