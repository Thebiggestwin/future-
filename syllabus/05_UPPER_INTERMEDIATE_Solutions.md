# 📚 UPPER-INTERMEDIATE DARAJA — Solutions Upper-Intermediate
**Kitob:** Solutions Upper-Intermediate (Oxford)  
**Muddat:** 4 oy (16 hafta)  
**Dars jadvali:** Haftada 3 marta × 2 soat = Haftada 6 soat  
**Jami soat:** ~96 soat

---

## 🎯 Daraja maqsadi
O'quvchi bu darajani tugatgach:
- Murakkab akademik va professional mavzularda gapira va yoza oladi
- 3rd Conditional, Mixed Conditionals, Advanced Passive, Inversion ni to'g'ri ishlatadi
- 2200-2500 ta so'z boyligiga ega bo'ladi
- IELTS formatidagi topshiriqlarda ishonch bilan ishlaydi
- CEFR: B2 → C1 darajasiga yetadi
- **IELTS maqsad: 6.5 — 7.0+**

---

| Hafta | Unit | Grammar | Vocabulary | Skills |

## 👨‍🏫 O'QITUVCHI UCHUN KO'RSATMALAR

1. **Writing:** Har haftada 1 ta to'liq essay — o'qituvchi batafsil izoh bilan qaytaradi
2. **Speaking:** IELTS Part 1, 2, 3 formatida mashq — har darsda 25-30 daqiqa
3. **Listening:** Haqiqiy IELTS audio materiallari ishlatiladi (Cambridge IELTS kitoblari)
4. **Reading:** Murakkab akademik matnlar — skimming va scanning texnikasi o'rgatiladi
5. **Vocabulary:** Collocations va idiomalar — kundalik foydalanish
6. **Mock test:** 15-haftada to'liq IELTS mock test — band score taxmin qilinadi
