# 📚 INTERMEDIATE DARAJA — Solutions Intermediate
**Kitob:** Solutions Intermediate (Oxford)  
**Muddat:** 4 oy (16 hafta)  
**Dars jadvali:** Haftada 3 marta × 2 soat = Haftada 6 soat  
**Jami soat:** ~96 soat

---

## 🎯 Daraja maqsadi
O'quvchi bu darajani tugatgach:
- Murakkab mavzularda erkin va ishonchli gapira oladi
- 2nd Conditional, Past Perfect, Passive Voice (barcha zamonlar) ni to'g'ri ishlatadi
- 1500-1800 ta so'z boyligiga ega bo'ladi
- Akademik uslubda paragraph va essay yoza oladi
- CEFR: B1 → B2 darajasiga yetadi

---

## 👨‍🏫 O'QITUVCHI UCHUN KO'RSATMALAR

1. **Writing:** Bu darajada essay yozish boshlanadiи — struktura (Introduction → Body → Conclusion) o'rgatiladi
2. **Speaking:** Debate va discussion — har darsda 20-25 daqiqa. O'quvchi o'z fikrini asoslay olishi kerak
3. **Vocabulary:** Academic Word List (AWL) dan kundalik foydalanish — har darsda 5-10 ta yangi so'z
4. **Grammar:** Xatolarni to'g'irlashda ehtiyot bo'ling — speaking paytida ko'p to'xtatmang, keyin yig'ma xato tahlili qiling
5. **Mock test:** 15-haftada to'liq IELTS formatiga o'xshash mock test o'tkazish tavsiya etiladi
6. **Upper-Int taklifi:** 16-haftada yakuniy test natijalaridan keyin har bir o'quvchiga individual taklif qiling

## 📌 MUHIM ESLATMA
> Bu daraja **B1 dan B2 ga** o'tish bosqichidir. Writing va Speaking ko'nikmalari bu yerda kuchli o'stirilmasa, IELTS da past ball chiqishi muqarrar. O'qituvchi bu ikki ko'nikmaga alohida e'tibor berishi shart.
