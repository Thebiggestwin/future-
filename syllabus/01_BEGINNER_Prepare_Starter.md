# 📚 BEGINNER DARAJA — Prepare Starter
**Kitob:** Prepare Starter (Cambridge)  
**Muddat:** 3 oy (12 hafta)  
**Dars jadvali:** Haftada 3 marta × 2 soat = Haftada 6 soat  
**Jami soat:** ~72 soat

---

## 🎯 Daraja maqsadi
O'quvchi bu darajani tugatgach:
- Oddiy so'zlashuv jumlalarini tushunadi va gapira oladi
- Asosiy grammatika tuzilmalarini biladi (to be, have got, present simple)
- 300-500 ta asosiy so'zni biladi
- O'zini tanishtira oladi, oddiy savollar bera oladi

---

## 📅 OYLIK PLAN

| Hafta | Unit | Grammar | Vocabulary | Skills |
|-------|------|---------|------------|--------|
| 1-hafta | Unit 1-2 | To be (am/is/are) | Numbers, colours, greetings | Listening: simple introductions |

## ⬆️ KEYINGI DARAJAGA O'TISH MEZONLARI

O'quvchi Elementary ga o'tishi uchun:
- ✅ Yakuniy testda **60+ ball** olgan bo'lishi kerak
- ✅ 3 oydan kamida **2 tasida** 60+ ball olgan bo'lishi kerak
- ✅ Darsga qatnashish **75%** dan kam bo'lmasligi kerak

---

## 👨‍🏫 O'QITUVCHI UCHUN KO'RSATMALAR

1. **Har darsda:** Vocabulary warm-up (5 daqiqa) bilan boshlang
2. **Grammar:** Avval tushuntiring → misollar → mashq → o'quvchi o'zi gapiradi
3. **Speaking:** Har darsda kamida 10 daqiqa suhbat (pair work)
4. **Uyga vazifa:** Har darsda beriladi — keyingi darsda tekshiriladi
5. **Zaif o'quvchilar:** Alohida qayd qilib, majburiy dars ro'yxatiga kiriting
6. **Kitob tempi:** Haftada 2 Unit — ortiq emas, kam emas

---
